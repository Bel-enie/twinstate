# Twinstate pitch deck

A short partnership pitch for university health services: the problem, five real-world cases, what Twinstate does, how it stays safe, where it stands, a proposed roadmap and the ask.

- `Twinstate-University-Pitch-Deck.pdf` — the deck, 14 slides, 16:9. The earlier hackathon deck for the judges stays at the repo root as `Twinstate-Pitch-Deck.pdf`, with its HTML in `docs/`.
- `source/` — the slide markup and shared styles the PDF was rendered from. Each slide is one `<section>` in `source/slides/`, ordered by `source/deck.json`. The screenshot used on the product slide is in `source/assets/`.

The roadmap slide is a draft for discussion. The closing slide has three placeholders to fill before sending: the sponsorship amount, its period, and the licence price.

Every figure about Twinstate comes from the project itself. The five cases cite their sources in the slide footers.
