06 · ROADMAP

DRAFT PROPOSAL

A proposed path onto campus

PHASE 1

Pilot

One semester with a volunteer student cohort, run through the health centre. Feedback comes by survey, because logs stay on students’ own devices.

PHASE 2

Integrate

Point every “ask a pharmacist” to your pharmacy, add your referral routes, and link the health centre’s own guidance into the evidence behind each flag.

PHASE 3

Licence

Roll out across the institution under an annual licence, with support, updates and onboarding for health-centre staff.

This roadmap is a proposal for discussion, not a commitment. Phases, timing and scope are open.
