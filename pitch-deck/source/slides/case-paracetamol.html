The same painkiller, twice

Acetaminophen, the paracetamol inside hundreds of products. United States.

The FDA found it was the leading cause of acute liver failure in the United States, and nearly half of those cases were accidental. About four in ten accidental cases involved two or more products containing it taken at once, such as a cold remedy on top of a painkiller. In 2011 the FDA capped the dose in prescription combination products.

WHAT WAS STACKED

A painkiller for a headache

+ A cold remedy with the same drug inside

THE GAP

Both labels were correct. Nothing added the two together.

Sources: FDA Drug Safety Communication, 2011; Michigan Medicine on the 2023 JAMA study.
