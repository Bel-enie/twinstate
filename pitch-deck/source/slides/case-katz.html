A heart condition and a lemonade

Sarah Katz, 21. University of Pennsylvania, 2022.

She had Long QT syndrome and had been advised to avoid energy drinks. She ordered a Panera Charged Lemonade, which was not sold as one, and a large cup held more caffeine than a Red Bull and a Monster together. She died hours later. Her family sued, Panera added warnings and then withdrew the drink, and the case settled in 2024.

WHAT WAS STACKED

A known heart condition

+ 390 mg of caffeine

THE GAP

Nothing connected the advice she had been given to the drink in her hand.

Sources: The Daily Pennsylvanian; CBS News, 2023–2024. Cause as alleged in the family’s lawsuit, which settled in 2024.
