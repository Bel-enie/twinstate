03 · HOW IT WORKS

From a log entry to an explained flag

STEP 1

Log

Prescriptions, painkillers, energy drinks, supplements and sleep.

STEP 2

Resolve

HOLON concept search maps each entry to a real clinical concept.

STEP 3

Screen

Every interaction is checked for severity, mechanism, effect and management.

STEP 4

Explain

Organ scores, a 0–100 interaction load and a plain-language next step, with the evidence attached.

The AI writes the explanation. Every score comes from the rule engine, and the model may only raise a severity, never lower it.
