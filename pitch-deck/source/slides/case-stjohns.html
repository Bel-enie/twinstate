A herbal remedy and the pill

St John’s wort, sold on any health-shop shelf. United Kingdom.

It lowers the blood level of the contraceptive pill. The UK medicines regulator has recorded unplanned pregnancies in women using it with the pill or the implant, and advised stopping it. Taken with common antidepressants such as sertraline, it can cause serotonin syndrome, which ranges from mild to life-threatening.
