01 · THE PROBLEM

Students take more than they realise

A prescription

From the campus clinic.

Painkillers

For a headache before a test.

Energy drinks

Two before a night of reading.

A herbal tonic

From home, on top of the rest.

Each one is harmless on its own, and nobody is tracking what they add up to.

The prescription is on file at the clinic. The painkillers, the energy drinks and the tonic are not.
