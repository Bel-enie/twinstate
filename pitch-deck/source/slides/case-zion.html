Even the hospital missed it

Libby Zion, 18, a college student. New York, 1984.

She was taking phenelzine, an MAOI antidepressant, which the hospital knew. For agitation she was given the painkiller meperidine. The two together are a known cause of serotonin syndrome, and she died within hours. The legal case was contested, but it showed most physicians were unaware of the interaction, and it changed New York’s law on resident working hours.
