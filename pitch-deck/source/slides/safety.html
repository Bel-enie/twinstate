04 · SAFETY

Safety and privacy are built in

Educational, not diagnostic

It explains what is known about what a student logs. It never diagnoses.

Urgent symptoms go to real care

A server-side red-flag list routes emergencies to real care, and no client can strip it.

Data stays in the browser

Everything a student logs stays on their own device. There is no account server.

Sources behind every flag

Each flag opens its records, the rule that fired and the NHS or FDA guidance behind it.
