Three drinks in two hours

Davis Cripe, 16. South Carolina, 2017.

A latte, a large Mountain Dew and an energy drink, in just under two hours. He had no heart condition and no other drugs or alcohol in his system. The coroner ruled a caffeine-induced cardiac event and stressed that it was not an overdose: the same amount on another day might have done nothing.

WHAT WAS STACKED

A latte

+ A large soda

+ An energy drink

THE GAP

No single drink was dangerous. Nobody was counting all three together.

Source: Richland County coroner, as reported by CBS News and Time, May 2017.
