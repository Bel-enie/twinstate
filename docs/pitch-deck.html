01 · Innovation

Not a form and a score. A body that reacts.

  • A living twin, not a report. A volumetric 3D body — beating heart, breathing chest, a scan beam sweeping it — that visibly changes colour as risk builds.
  • AI calculates the numbers from your data, not a fixed formula: organ scores and a 0–100 body-stress index reasoned from what you actually logged.
  • Two futures for the same body. A What-If coach projects "keep this pattern" against "rest + hydrate" over the coming weeks.
02 · Clinical value

The exact harm students never see: stacking.

Exam season runs on caffeine, paracetamol, ibuprofen and "study aids" — taken together, without anyone adding up the load. Twinstate is the drug-safety check for that reality, with a clear path to real care.

Consent gate, "not a diagnosis" disclaimers on every screen, and urgent-symptom routing straight to real care.

RESILIENCE

Graceful fallbacks

HOLON → AI → offline engine. A flaky venue network degrades the source, never the demo. Plus an app-wide error boundary.

CONSISTENCY

Risk scores, the 3D twin, history and simulation all recompute from one assessment — they can never drift apart.

04 · Use of the platform

Real HOLON, deeply wired.

  • HOLON clinical knowledge — the real @ontomorph/holon-client: concepts.search() resolves every substance, interactions.checkList() screens the whole list.
  • 3D anatomy — the twin is the emotional centrepiece; organs are coloured and animated by the risk HOLON + AI compute.
  • AI reasoning — calculates the organ scores + body-stress index and powers a twin-grounded health chat.
  • The twin model — a body sliced into systems, each carrying the events (doses) and findings (flags) pinned to it.
